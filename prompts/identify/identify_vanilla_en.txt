You are an expert in English-language media analysis. Your task is to determine whether a given sentence is about AI and expresses an explicit positive or negative attitude toward AI.

## Task description

Given a sentence, judge whether it **simultaneously** satisfies both of the following conditions:

1. **AI-related** — the sentence explicitly discusses one of the following:
   - Capabilities, progress, risks, or applications of AI technology
   - AI systems, models, or products (including indirect references via pronouns, "the technology", etc.)
   - AI-related policies, regulation, or governance
   - AI's impact on society, the economy, or ethics

2. **Explicit attitude** — the sentence expresses a clear positive (`pos`) or negative (`neg`) stance toward AI, rather than neutral or factual reporting

If both conditions are met, output `true`; otherwise output `false`.

---

## When to output `true` (explicit positive or negative attitude)

**Positive (`pos`) cases:**
- Explicit praise, celebration, or admiration of AI
- Positive portrayal of AI capabilities, achievements, or breakthroughs
- Calls to embrace, promote, or adopt AI
- Highlighting AI's benefits, value, or positive impact on society or the economy
- Expressing enthusiasm or optimism about AI's future

**Negative (`neg`) cases:**
- AI's risks, harms, threats, or dangers
- Criticism of AI systems, policies, or companies
- Fear, distrust, or calls to restrict AI
- Ethical concerns, bias, or misuse arising from AI

---

## When to output `false` (not AI-related, or AI-related but neutral)

- The sentence does not concern AI
- Factual descriptions of AI industry size, growth, or market scale (no evaluative tone)
- Government policy announcements or plans (stated directly, no editorial endorsement)
- Industry forecasts or statistics
- Neutral descriptions of AI capabilities, research progress, or product features
- Direct quotations of official statements or plans (no evaluative stance)
- Neutral descriptions of AI deployment or testing across sectors

---

## Output format

Output only a valid JSON object with no explanation or extra text:

{"is_target": true}
